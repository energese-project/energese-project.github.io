Research

The software exists because a question needed settling, and settling it required a kernel that could actually run the models.

Three Accountings, One Model

Reconciling Odum’s Dynamics, Emergy, and IFRS/AASB Financial Reporting — Sholto Maud. Companion to ADR 0009.

A model is a system of ordinary differential equations describing flows of energy, matter and money through a network of storages. Over that same network, emergy accounting values those flows by the energy embodied in producing them, under an algebra of its own. A general ledger is a third thing again: discrete, exactly balancing entries recognised under a body of accounting standards. The premise is that one artefact can serve all three purposes without deforming any of them.

The reconciliation turns on a single algebraic distinction. The quantities travelling a pathway divide into extensive ones, which add over subsystems and enter the dynamics as summands, and intensive ratios, which do not add and enter as multiplicands. Because zero is the identity for addition and the annihilator for multiplication, that one test decides both what may be posted to a ledger and where a kernel may supply a default.

The organising resolution is that a simulated trajectory is evidence from which entries are derived, not the authoritative record itself. A numerical solver is accurate to a stated tolerance; a ledger must balance to the last cent. The two stay separate artefacts joined by an explicit conversion procedure.

Where it does not hold

The argument covers conservative extensive carriers — mass, energy, goods, money — and not carriers that are consumed rather than transferred, or that can be minted and burned. And the kernel implements the half of the emergy algebra that breaks additivity without the rule that bounds it: on any topology where co-product pathways recombine, it overstates transformity by a factor equal to the number of reuniting branches. It is a faithful simulator of Odum’s dynamics, and it is not yet a faithful implementation of his emergy algebra. That is stated here rather than left to be discovered.

What it costs

A consumer who wants books runs the conversion procedure themselves, because the kernel will not. Within a period already reported, the endogenous pricing that lets inflation emerge from the model must be switched off, and prices there are historical data. Revenue recognised at a single instant rather than progressively cannot presently be expressed at all. Emergy is excluded from the ledger permanently rather than pending further work — a consequence of arithmetic, not of the standards omitting it.

How to refute it

Every quantitative claim is the output of a program, and the program is public. Where the document says a number comes out one way, that is checkable by running it, and someone who makes it come out another way has refuted the claim rather than disagreed with it. The central negative result — a disagreement with Campbell’s 2013 construction on one of its three parts — is stated in exactly that form, with the experiment that would overturn it.
